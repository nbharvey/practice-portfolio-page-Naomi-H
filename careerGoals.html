My Journey from Education to Software

I started my career in education, driven by a love for learning and problem-solving. Over time, I found myself increasingly drawn to the world of software engineering—where creativity, logic, and innovation collide. With support from my brother and a strong self-taught foundation, I made the leap into tech.

Endurance and Adventure

From half marathons and triathlons to rock climbing and bike races, I'm constantly pushing my limits. My mission? To visit all U.S. national parks and take on epic backcountry adventures. Endurance sports teach me focus, resilience, and adaptability—skills that translate beautifully into the tech world.

Plans for my future career

As someone who thrives in the outdoors, I've experienced firsthand the impact of climate change on our trails, oceans, and ecosystems. As I've learned more about what careers are possible in tech, I've come to understand that software is an essential piece of the puzzle when it comes to scientific discoveries about our Earth. I am interesed in exploring software in learning about using data to support environmental science, marine conservation, and climate resilience. I see software not just as a career, but as a tool to protect the planet I love to explore.

Steps I need to take to further my career goals:

  1. Graduate LaunchCode!
  2. Build a portfolio project that combines data, climate, and UI (e.g., national park climate tracker)
  3. Contribute to an open-source project related to environmental data or public impact
  4. Learn and practice data visualization tools (like D3.js, Leaflet, or Tableau)
  5. Apply to software engineering jobs with climate-focused or mission-driven companies
  6. Network with others in climate tech via LinkedIn and tech meetups
  7. Keep endurance training and document my journey as a unique blend of tech and nature
