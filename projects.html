Triathlon Tracker

The application I'm imagining is an app for athletes to track their training plans, for example, triathletes. The application will be able to track each workout (swim, bike, run) and the mileage and duration of each workout per week. It will also be able to track the workout you are doing on a particular day, and if you skip the workout or move it for whatever reason, the database can re-render and adjust for that automatically. It will also create your workout plan for you based on an algorithm, and the workout will become increasingly longer and more complex per week.
